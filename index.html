02 Add the transcript

This is what the AI reads to find highlights. Timestamped lines like [01:23] what was said let it cut precisely — plain text works too, just less precisely.

Free, runs on your device (~80MB model, first time only). Works for uploaded files and direct video URLs — not YouTube links.
Or get a transcript some other way
  • YouTube — below the video, open the "···" menu → Show transcript, then copy the text.
  • Zoom, podcast hosts, meeting tools — most can export one automatically.
  • Nothing available? Paste your own rough notes with approximate timestamps.
